Design System

A Decade of Rain

Warm paper cartography, forest-green ink, and one orange accent family. Orange draws, forest speaks.

Colour

One warm-paper ground, one forest ink, one orange accent tuned per surface so every text pairing clears WCAG AA. Contrast ratios are measured against the ground each colour sits on.

Vietnamese diacritics — the reason for the sans choice

Đà Nẵng · Biên Hòa · Phú Cát · Cà Mau · A Lưới · Sài Gòn Public Sans carries the full Vietnamese subset, so UI text and map glyphs share one face with no fallback mixing.
04

Map markers

Two families, one system. A point names a place; an area names a region. Live examples below (ring and outline animate; reduced-motion holds them still).

Đắk Tô

Point · .map-dot

Solid --accent dot (12px, white halo) + pulsing ring, plus a --accent-chip label. Variants: above (default), below, or led on a hairline (leader).

Cà Mau

Area · .map-area-label

The pulsing --accent-line boundary is the pointer, so the chip floats alone at the anchor. No pointer triangle, no dot.

05

Motion

Every animation below is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — keep that invariant when adding new motion.

Map first paintHolds at opacity 0 behind the hook banner, fades in ~0.35s once tiles and data are ready. Catches up to the current node if the reader scrolled early.
Spray bloomOn each Act I step the heat filter’s day threshold is animated (easeOutCubic, ~1.5–2.8s) from the previous event to the new one, so the sprayed area grows in chronological order. Starts on camera arrival; throttled to ~60 filter re-applies.
Hook rainPlays at the top, fades out ~1.4s after half a viewport of scroll, parks, fades back in ~0.8s at the very top.
PulsesDot ring (CSS, 2.2s); landmark outline breathes on an rAF sine, opacity 0.5–0.95.
Act II revealsCard face cross-fade, alternatives check pop, methods open-grid fade, timeline card fade-in, closing rain.

Rail resting links are a recorded AA exception: 11px white on the lighter orange-red gradient top measures ≈3.2:1; the active item (white on the highlight fill, bold) passes.
