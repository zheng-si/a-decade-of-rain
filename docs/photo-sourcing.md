# Interlude photo credits — final ledger

Both Interlude photo walls now use **only** public-domain (U.S. military /
NARA) or Creative-Commons (Wikimedia Commons) images. No copyrighted press
photos (Nicolai, NYT, LIFE, Getty, age fotostock) remain. The piece is
publishable and enterable for awards without a licensing liability.

Before launch, open each Wikimedia Commons file page and copy its exact
license line verbatim into the `credit` field in
`src/content/interlude/consequences.ts`.
